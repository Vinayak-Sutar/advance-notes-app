Everything here is saved on your laptop, in plain files. Nothing leaves it unless a feature below says so.

## Saving something

Type anything in the box and press Enter. A thought, a link, a picture. The app files it for you.

- Say **remind me friday at 5 to call mom** and it sets a reminder.
- Say **add to the trip note that we need sunscreen** and it adds to that note.
- Say **exam prep list: algebra, calculus by 15 nov** and it makes a to-do list with a deadline.
- Start with **?** to search your notes.
- Press **Tab** to switch the box between Save and Ask.

On your phone, tap **＋**. Your keyboard's mic button works there too.

## To-do lists

- Say **exam prep list: algebra, calculus by 15 nov**, or press **New list** on the **To-do lists** page.
- Tick items on the list or right on its tile. Done ones fold away; tap **N done** to see them again.
- Lines starting with `## ` make sections, each with its own count.
- The deadline chip shows the days left and whether you're on track. Tap it to move the deadline.
- The bell beside an item sets a reminder for it, shown as a small chip.
- A list with a deadline checks in once a day with how it's going. Tap its check-in chip to change the time, pause it or turn it off, or say **don't remind me about exam prep until monday**.
- In the box or in chat, say **add optics to the exam prep list**.

## Finding things

- **Today** shows what needs you today, including dated items from your lists, and how each list is going.
- **Reminders** shows everything with a time, day by day.
- **All notes** shows everything, and **Inbox** holds what the app wasn't sure about. One tap files it.
- **Collections** appear by themselves as you save more.
- **List** or **Tiles** switches how notes look. Drag tiles into your own order.
- On your phone, the magnifier at the top searches, and the round button next to it opens everything else.

## Writing in a note

Click a line and type. Markdown works: `## heading`, `- list`, `- [ ] to-do`.

- Drag the dots beside a line to move it. A heading takes its whole section with it.
- Tap **Remind me** on a note to give it a time.

## Asking the AI

The AI runs on your laptop, so your notes stay there.

- **Chat** answers from your notes and shows which ones it used. It can search the web for recent facts: only your search words leave the laptop, never your notes.
- **The AI box in a note** answers questions, or changes the note when you ask: "add the colours", "make this a checklist".
- **Think harder** makes it slower and more careful.

## Personal notes

Your saved WhatsApp chats, organised: summaries, key points, lists like Books and Movies to watch, and links as tiles. Click a tile to open the link, or ⋯ for details. Drag tiles to arrange them.

This section only displays. It's updated when new files are processed, not from the page. Private details are hidden, and open only on the laptop.

## Your phone

1. Double-click the Notes shortcut on your laptop.
2. Scan the QR code in its window with your phone. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
3. To keep it handy, use **Add to Home Screen** in your browser's menu.

On the phone, pull down on a list to refresh it, and swipe down on a panel's top to close it. Opened from the Home Screen, swipe in from the left edge to go back.

You only scan once per phone. The code changes each time Notes starts, so a stranger who saw an old one can't use it. **Settings, General** on the laptop shows the code again and lists your phones, where you can remove one.

The laptop has to be on, with the Notes window open. Your notes stay on the laptop; the phone is a window onto them. Private details in Personal notes, and the AI's settings, open only on the laptop.

## Profiles

Each profile is a separate set of notes: for example one for real use and one for trying things out. To switch, use the profile button at the bottom of the sidebar, or on your phone the round button at the top. They're completely separate, and each device remembers its own choice.

## Your notes are safe

- Every note is a plain file in the `profiles` folder.
- A dated backup is made each day, and the last 7 are kept in `backups`.
- **Ctrl+Z** or **Undo** reverses most changes.
